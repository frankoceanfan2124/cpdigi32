Grounded in research

Rather than guessing at what makes a group work well, this project draws on five sources covering both practical workplace advice and peer-reviewed research — including Hackman and Wageman's six conditions for high-performing teams.

What causes poor dynamics
Weak leadership, groupthink, unclear goals, and a lack of trust show up again and again across the research.
What the research recommends
Clear roles, open communication, psychological safety, and shared values consistently separate strong teams from struggling ones.
The academic backbone
Hackman & Wageman's six conditions — three "Essentials" and three "Enablers" — anchor the practical advice in real research.
