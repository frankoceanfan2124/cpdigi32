what good group dynamics actually looks like

Grounded in real research

I didn't want to just guess at what makes a group work well, so I read through five sources before building this site. A mix of practical workplace advice, plus one that's actually backed by peer reviewed research: Hackman and Wageman's six conditions for high performing teams.

  • What causes poor dynamics: weak leadership, groupthink, unclear goals, and a lack of trust show up again and again across the research.
  • What the research recommends: clear roles, open communication, psychological safety, and shared values consistently separate strong teams from struggling ones.
  • The academic backbone: Hackman & Wageman's six conditions, three "Essentials" and three "Enablers", anchor the practical advice in real research.
